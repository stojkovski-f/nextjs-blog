## Annual report

Review **income statement**, **balance sheet**, and **cash flow**. Focus on:

- Association **interest-bearing debt** and rate periods
- **Maintenance fund** vs upcoming projects
- Planned **major investments** (pipes, facade, elevator)

## Maintenance plan

A credible **maintenance plan** shows when large works are due. If big projects lack funding, **monthly fees** may rise sharply.

## Bylaws and disclaimers

Check **share size**, **transfer fees**, and **inspection disclaimers**. Ask the board about disputes or planned fee increases.

## Red flags

- Negative cash flow for years without a plan
- Very low fee that does not cover operations
- Deferred maintenance on an aging building
