## Down payment (kontantinsats)

You typically need at least a **15 percent down payment** of the purchase price (rules and exceptions apply). Banks expect **own funds** or approved financing — not always another unsecured loan.

Also budget for **transaction costs**, moving, and a **post-completion buffer**.

## Skuldkvot (debt-to-income)

**Skuldkvot** is total debt (including the new mortgage) divided by **gross annual income**. Swedish banks and Finansinspektionen use it as a key limit — often around **4.5–6** depending on price and profile.

Example: household income SEK 900,000/year and total debt SEK 4,050,000 → skuldkvot 4.5.

## Stress testing

Banks often calculate affordability at a **higher interest rate** than today’s list rate. Keep savings after completion — many advisers suggest several months of living costs.

## Checklist

- [ ] Down payment plus fees available
- [ ] Skuldkvot within bank guidelines
- [ ] No hidden consumer credit eating capacity
