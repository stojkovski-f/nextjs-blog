A **bolån** (mortgage) is a loan secured against your home. For most first-time buyers it is the largest debt they will take on — so understanding the terms before bidding pays off.

## Interest and monthly cost

- **Nominal rate** is the bank’s headline rate; **effective rate** includes fees.
- You can choose a **variable rate** (tracks the market) or a **fixed rate** for a **binding period**.
- Monthly cost depends on interest, **amortization**, and any **bank fees**.

## Binding period

The binding period is how long your agreed rate applies. Longer fixes often mean a higher rate but more predictability. Many borrowers split the loan across different periods.

## Pre-approval and loan-to-value

Banks issue **lånelöfte** (pre-approval) based on income, existing debt, and buffer savings. **Loan-to-value** is the loan relative to the property value — higher leverage triggers stricter amortization rules (see the budget article).

## Practical steps

1. Gather payslips and an overview of savings and debts.
2. Compare pre-approval and rate offers from at least two banks.
3. Stress-test monthly cost at today’s rate and at higher rates.
