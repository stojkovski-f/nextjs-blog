## Support and incentives

Rules and programs **change** — always verify with Skatteverket, your municipality, and your bank. First-time buyers have historically benefited from:

- **Ränteavdrag** (interest cost deduction on home loans)
- Municipal **housing queues** (not available everywhere)
- Banks’ **starter** products or amortization exceptions in some cases (terms vary)

## Myths to challenge

| Myth | Reality |
|------|---------|
| "I need 20% down" | Often 15%, but banks can require more individually |
| "Variable rate is always cheapest" | Depends on rate outlook and how long you stay |
| "The monthly fee doesn’t matter" | Co-op fees are a major part of housing cost |
