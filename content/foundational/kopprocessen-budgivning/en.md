## Before you bid

1. **Pre-approval** in place and max price set (including fees).
2. Review **inspection**, association documents, and energy declaration.
3. Ask the broker how bidding works (open, sealed, etc.).

## Bid and contract

When your **bid** is accepted, the purchase agreement is **binding** — read terms carefully. **Handpenning** (deposit) is paid per contract; the remainder at **completion**.

## Completion (tillträde)

At completion you pay the balance, the bank disburses the loan, and you receive **keys**. Arrange **insurance** and utilities from completion day.

## Common mistakes

- Bidding above budget without a rate stress test
- Skipping review of association loans and maintenance plan
- Forgetting registration fees and moving costs
