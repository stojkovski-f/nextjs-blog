## Bostadsrätt (tenant-owner co-op)

You buy a **share** in a **housing cooperative** — you do not own the building outright. You pay a **monthly fee** (avgift) for operations, association loans, and maintenance. The bank still secures the loan against your apartment.

**Pros:** often lower entry price in cities, less building maintenance on you.  
**Cons:** dependent on association finances; fees can rise.

## House (villa / småhus)

You own the **property** (leasehold or freehold). You pay for **maintenance**, insurance, and major repairs.

**Pros:** control; no association fee (but all costs are yours).  
**Cons:** higher and less predictable upkeep.

## Lending and valuation

The bank values the unit and, for co-ops, the association. Weak associations can mean worse terms or stricter amortization.

Read the due diligence article before signing on a bostadsrätt.
