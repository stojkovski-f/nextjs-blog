## More than interest alone

Your **housing cost** includes:

- Mortgage interest and **amortization**
- **Monthly fee** (co-op) or running costs (house)
- Electricity, insurance, broadband
- **Parking** or storage if applicable

## Amortization requirements

Sweden has **amorteringskrav** tied to loan-to-value and skuldkvot. Higher leverage means mandatory yearly amortization — lower risk for the bank, higher monthly cost for you from day one.

## Buffer and surprises

Budget for **maintenance** (especially older homes), association fee changes, and rate hikes. Simple stress test: add **2 percentage points** to your rate and check the budget.
