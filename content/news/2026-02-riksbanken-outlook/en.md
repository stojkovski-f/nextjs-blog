The **Riksbank’s** latest communication affects both variable list rates and **fixed-rate** offers. For households with mortgages:

1. **New loans** — banks price off policy and market rate expectations.
2. **Refinancing** — compare break costs on existing fixes vs a new fixed period.
3. **Amortization** — rules unchanged, but higher rates increase real monthly cost.

Stockholm buyers often face a **higher skuldkvot** due to price levels — plan an extra buffer.
