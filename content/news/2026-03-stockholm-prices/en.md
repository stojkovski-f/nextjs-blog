After a quieter period, **Stockholm’s housing market** shows signs of stabilizing. Latest quarterly indicators suggest:

- **Inner city** — flatter price levels, longer time on market in some segments.
- **Commuter suburbs** — more deals and somewhat stronger demand from first-time buyers.
- **Rates** — less volatility than prior years, so more buyers consider fixed-rate periods.

## If you are buying

First-time buyers should still focus on **total monthly cost** rather than a single listing discount. Compare association finances and stress-test your mortgage at **+1–2 percentage points** interest.
